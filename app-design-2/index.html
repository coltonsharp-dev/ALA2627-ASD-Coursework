How this course works

1

One repo, all year

Your own copy of the class repo. Every assignment is a new folder you pull into it — the work accumulates instead of scattering.

2

Build in the Codespace

Everything runs in the browser. Nothing to install, and the same environment every day.

3

Choose your agent mode

Five words decide how much help you take. You record which one you used in the commit.

4

Turn in your link

Your live URL, once. It never changes — the work behind it grows.

The agent

Five words: EXPLAIN, REVIEW, DEBUG, PAIR, SPEC. Start a message with one and the agent works that way for the reply — you are choosing how much of the thinking you keep. The prompts and the full explanation are on this week’s page, and the mode you used goes at the end of every commit.

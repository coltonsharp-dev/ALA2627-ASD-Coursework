How this works

Open the day

Each day has its own page: what you are learning, the plan, the files, and a place to try it before you build.

Pull them into your Codespace with one line in the terminal, or download the folder. Both are on the day page.

Turn in your link

Submit the live URL of your page. Not a file. The page itself is what gets graded.

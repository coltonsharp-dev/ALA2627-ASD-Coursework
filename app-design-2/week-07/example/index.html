Colton Sharp

I teach students to build software and robots that actually run — and I build the tools they learn on.

What I do

I teach five CTE courses in Room 703: JH Robotics, CTE Robotics I, App Design 1 and 2, and JH Technology I. My students work the way working developers do — in their own GitHub repositories, in Codespaces, publishing live pages from the first weeks of the year.

I build the coursework platform they use, because the curriculum for these pathways mostly did not exist and a table of lesson titles is not a course.

What I build

A coursework site

Every lesson as its own page: what you are learning, the plan, the files, and something to try before you build. Students pull each assignment into their own repo with one line.

Week packs that can be taught from

Forty-three of them. Lesson plans, whiteboard cards, vocabulary, student handouts and an answer key — a week a substitute could walk in and run.

Class pages for seven classrooms

One page per classroom, each carrying the year, the current week, and every file that goes with it.

What I teach with

Software HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Students ship real pages, publicly, from week one.
Tooling Git, GitHub, Codespaces Version control as a habit rather than a unit at the end of the year.
Robotics VEX IQ and VEX V5 Build, program, sense, compete — in that order, because it is the order it works in.
